Projects

Unispeak

Motivation

Language learning apps like Duolingo offer structured lessons, but they often fall short when it comes to real-world application. I wanted to help bridge that gap by designing a platform that connects native speakers with beginner speakers to practice language through genuine immersion and conversation rather than repetitive drills.

Functionality

Unispeak pairs users based on their language goals and connects them for real-world speech practice. Instead of scripted lessons, users engage in natural conversation, making the learning experience more practical and effective.

Project Design

This was a user-centered design project. The process began with low-fidelity wireframes and progressed to high-fidelity interactive prototypes built in Figma. Every design decision was grounded in user research and feedback to ensure the experience felt intuitive and accessible.

My Role

I led the prototyping process in Figma, designing both the low and high fidelity mockups. My focus was on creating a clean, user-friendly interface that kept the immersive language experience at the center of every interaction.

Seven Segment Display

Motivation

This project was an opportunity to work at the intersection of hardware and software. A 7-segment display is an electronic component that uses seven individual LEDs arranged in a figure-eight shape to display numerical digits and characters. I wanted to bring that concept to life by building it from scratch.

Functionality

The display outputs the digits 0–9 and characters A, B, C, D, E, and F based on physical switch inputs on an Arduino Nano board. Each combination of switches triggers a specific segment pattern, lighting up the correct LED configuration to form the corresponding number or letter.

Project Design & Implementation

I wrote all of the logic to map switch inputs to their corresponding segment outputs, then uploaded the code to the Arduino Nano and wired everything into the circuit schematic. This project gave me hands-on experience with embedded systems and low-level hardware programming.

Transact Nutritional App

Motivation

As a college student, staying on top of nutrition can be tough. Existing apps like MyFitnessPal are powerful but can feel overwhelming. I wanted to help build something simpler and more accessible specifically designed for college students who want to make healthier choices without the complexity.

Functionality

Transact allows users to take a photo of a piece of fruit and instantly receive its nutritional information. The app tracks meal history and gives users a clear picture of what they are consuming day to day.

Project Design & Implementation

The app was built using React on the frontend and Node.js on the backend. I primarily focused on the frontend, developing the login flow, user profile, and overall application experience. My goal was to keep the interface clean and intuitive so that any student could pick it up and start using it right away.
